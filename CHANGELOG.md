## [0.0.1] - 2026-10-24

### Added

- Nine light themes: Purple, Red, Pink, Yellow, Green, Blue, Blue Gray, Coffee Cream and Cream. Purple is the source; the other eight are generated from it by rotating the purple hue band, with per-variant saturation and paleness.
  ライトテーマ 9 色(Purple・Red・Pink・Yellow・Green・Blue・Blue Gray・Coffee Cream・Cream)。Purple を原本に、紫の色相帯を回転して他の 8 色を生成。色ごとに彩度と淡さの調整あり
- Extension icon: a shallow dish holding five pastel bars of soap.
  拡張アイコン: 浅いお皿にパステルの石けんが 5 つ
- Released under GPL-3.0 as a derivative of Bearded Theme (BeardedBear) via its Milkshake Blueberry variant. See README for credits.
  Bearded Theme(BeardedBear)の Milkshake Blueberry を元にした派生物として GPL-3.0 で公開。出典は README を参照
